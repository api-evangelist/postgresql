Tainan, Taiwan - Sun 12 Jul 2026 powa-archivist 5.2.0 The PoWA team is pleased to announce the release of the version 5.2.0 of powa-archivist , the core extension of the PoWA project. PoWA (PostgreSQL Workload Analyzer) is a performance tool, compatible with all supported PostgreSQL versions. It allows to collect and aggregate metrics gathered from multiple PostgreSQL instances using various extensions covering all parts of PostgreSQL and provides real-time charts and graphs to help monitor and tune your servers.
