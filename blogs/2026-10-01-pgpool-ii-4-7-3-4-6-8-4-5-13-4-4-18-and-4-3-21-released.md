What is Pgpool-II? Pgpool-II is a tool to add useful features to PostgreSQL, including: connection pooling load balancing automatic failover and more . Minor releases Pgpool Global Development Group is pleased to announce the availability of following versions of Pgpool-II: 4.7.3 4.6.8 4.5.13 4.4.18 4.3.21 These releases include security fixes.
