Autobase 2.11 is an important milestone in the evolution of the platform. Two years ago, we started working on a broader goal: evolve the Autobase Console from a PostgreSQL deployment UI into a platform for managing the complete cluster lifecycle. With this release, the remaining major pieces of that roadmap are now in place - scaling, upgrades, backups, and point-in-time recovery can all be managed directly from the Platform UI.
