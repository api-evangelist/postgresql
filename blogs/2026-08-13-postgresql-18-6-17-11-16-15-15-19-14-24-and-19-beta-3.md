The PostgreSQL Global Development Group has released an update to all supported versions of PostgreSQL, including 18.6, 17.11, 16.15, 15.19, and 14.24, as well as the third beta release of PostgreSQL 19. This release fixes 28 security vulnerabilities and over 110 bugs reported over the last several months. This release skips PostgreSQL 18 versions from PostgreSQL 18.4 to 18.6.
